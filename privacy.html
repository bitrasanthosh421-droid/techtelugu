Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Tech Telugu Hub (mycoinrush.online)

At Tech Telugu Hub (accessible from mycoinrush.online), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This document details the types of information recorded and how we handle user data.

1. Log Files

Like most web servers, our hosting infrastructure utilizes log files. These records include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. None of this data is linked to personally identifiable information; its sole purpose is analyzing traffic trends and administering the portal securely.

2. Cookies & Web Beacons

Tech Telugu Hub uses cookies to store visitor preferences and optimize user experience based on browser type. Third-party vendors, including Google AdSense, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve relevant advertisements based on visits to this and other websites across the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out portal.

3. Third-Party Links

Our directory contains hyperlinks to external web tools, app stores, and utility platforms. Once you click an external link, you leave our domain. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit, as we have no control over their practices.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove it.

5. Contact Information

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact our compliance officer at legal@mycoinrush.online.
