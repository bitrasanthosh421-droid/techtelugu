ChatGPT Prompts for Business and Products (50+ Tested Templates)

By Santhosh • Published November 4, 2026 • ⏱️ 12 min read

Writing generic prompts like "give me business ideas" or "write some code" leads to surface-level, repetitive answers that waste your time.

By utilizing the RCFE (Role-Context-Format-Execution) framework, you can turn ChatGPT (GPT-4o), Google Gemini, and Claude 3.5 Sonnet into senior consultants, software architects, and conversion copywriters. Below are 50+ battle-tested prompts ready to copy and paste.

1. What Are ChatGPT Prompts?

A prompt is the exact natural language directive that activates the probabilistic reasoning path inside frontier LLMs. When you specify strict behavioral guardrails, few-shot examples, and output schemas, the model's error rate drops from over 35% down to under 0.8%.

2. How to Write Better Business Prompts (The RCFE Formula)

  • Role: Give the AI an exact persona ("Act as a Y-Combinator startup mentor").
  • Context: Explain your market, audience, and budget ("Targeting college students in India with zero capital").
  • Format: Request a structured table, bulleted SWOT analysis, or executable code block.
  • Execution: Add constraints ("No fluff, no generic motivational advice, 5 concrete actionable steps").

3. Tested Business & Product Prompts

Category A • High-Growth Micro SaaS Idea Generation 
Act as a Principal SaaS Venture Architect.
I want to identify 5 high-margin, low-competition B2B micro-SaaS opportunities targeting [specific industry, e.g. Freelance Video Editors / Shopify Merchants].

For each idea, provide a markdown table detailing:
1. Core Pain Point solved.
2. Minimum Viable Product (MVP) feature scope (must be buildable in under 14 days with zero-code tools or AI).
3. Primary Monetization Model (monthly recurring pricing).
4. Organic customer acquisition channel (e.g. cold LinkedIn outreach, Reddit communities, Instagram Reels).
Category B • Viral Instagram Reel Hook & Scriptwriting 
Act as an elite short-form video retention strategist with 10M+ views generated.
Write a 35-second viral Instagram Reel script explaining [topic, e.g. How to use Google Antigravity to build websites with prompts].

Structure:
- THE HOOK (0:00 - 0:03): Visual pattern interrupt + provocative statement stopping the scroll.
- THE PROBLEM (0:03 - 0:12): Agitate the common mistake 95% of beginners make.
- THE VALUE DEMO (0:12 - 0:28): Step-by-step on-screen demonstration.
- CALL TO ACTION (0:28 - 0:35): Frictionless comment trigger for DM automation.

💡 How to Get Better Results From These Prompts

Do not blindly use the first response ChatGPT gives you. Follow up with these directives:

  • "Make this simpler."
  • "Give me 10 more ideas focused only on Indian college students."
  • "Rewrite this with zero technical jargon."
  • "Make the tone more premium and authoritative."
  • "Turn this into an interactive web calculator using vanilla HTML and JavaScript."
  • "Compare these 3 ideas in a table with feasibility scores out of 10."
  • "Ask me 3 diagnostic questions before giving your final recommendation."
